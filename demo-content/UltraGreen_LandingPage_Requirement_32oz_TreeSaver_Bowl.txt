PRODUCT NAME
------------
32 oz TreeSaver™ Bowl

ABOUT / WHAT IT DOES
---------------------
A sustainable food-service bowl made from 100% renewable natural
resources — a fiber base with PLA lamination that keeps oils and liquids
from soaking through, so it holds up for pasta, soup, salads, and sushi in
both dine-in and takeout/delivery settings. It's oven-safe (to 425°F),
microwave-safe, and freezer-safe, and composts in a commercial composter
in 60–90 days after use. Sized at 32 oz, it's UltraGreen's most versatile
bowl — the one operators reach for across the widest range of menu items.

WHY IT MATTERS / PRIMARY BENEFIT
-----------------------------------
Fewer SKUs to stock, fewer containers that fail mid-service, and a
genuine sustainability story to put in front of customers — without
sacrificing the performance a busy kitchen needs (leak-proof, oven-to-
freezer range).
