UltraGreen Sustainable Packaging & Products — Marketing SOP
==============================================================

1. WHO WE ARE
--------------
UltraGreen Sustainable Packaging & Products manufactures earth-friendly,
renewable food packaging for the food service industry — pizzerias, sushi
restaurants, catering operations, delis, and other restaurants that need
compostable takeout and dine-in containers. Our mission is to be the
premier manufacturer of sustainable, earth-friendly food packaging made
from renewable resources, and to become a worldwide leader in
biodegradable, compostable food packaging.

Brand tagline: "Be sustainable."
Category tagline: "Sustainable packaging for your clients."

2. BRAND VOICE
--------------
- Tone: professional yet approachable. We are the reliable, knowledgeable
  supplier — not preachy or guilt-tripping about sustainability.
- Balance two things in every message: environmental credentials AND
  practical, everyday performance (freezable, microwaveable, leak-proof,
  oven-safe). Never sell eco-friendliness alone — always pair it with a
  functional benefit a busy kitchen actually cares about.
- Speak to operators (restaurant owners, kitchen managers, caterers), not
  end consumers — they're buying by the case, thinking about cost,
  durability, and whether it'll hold up in a delivery bag.
- Keep claims accurate and specific. Prefer concrete numbers ("composts in
  60–90 days in a commercial composter", "oven-safe to 425°F") over vague
  claims ("eco-friendly", "green") used on their own.

3. MATERIALS & CLAIMS — USE CAREFULLY
--------------------------------------
- Core materials: sugarcane fiber (bagasse), bamboo, and other tree-free,
  renewable plant fiber, often with a PLA (plant-based plastic) lining for
  leak/oil resistance. Recycled PET is used for clear lids.
- FDA approved. Certified compostable.
- Compostability claim to use: "commercially compostable" — always mention
  "commercial composter" rather than implying backyard composting unless a
  specific product page confirms that.
- Do not claim "recyclable" for the fiber bowls themselves — only the clear
  PET lids are recyclable. Keep these two claims separate and correct.

4. PREFERRED LANGUAGE
----------------------
- CTA verbs: "Shop Now", "Request a Sample", "Get the Catalog", "Switch to Sustainable".
- Avoid: "eco warrior", "save the planet", "guilt-free" — too preachy for a
  B2B food-service audience.
- Lead with the functional benefit relevant to the product (leak-proof,
  oven-safe, freezer-safe, holds up in delivery), THEN the sustainability
  story, then the CTA.
- When in doubt, favor a confident, solutions-first tone: "durable enough
  for delivery, kind enough for the planet" rather than sustainability-only
  framing.

5. VISUAL DIRECTION
--------------------
- Product photography should feel clean, natural, and food-forward — real
  food in the container (pasta, salad, sushi, soup), warm natural light,
  minimal props, kraft/green color accents reflecting the plant-based
  materials.
- No text or logos baked directly into generated images — captions/CTAs are
  added separately in the app.

6. THIS SOP APPLIES TO
------------------------
Every post and landing page generated for UltraGreen, across every
platform and product line — not just whichever product is being featured
in a specific generation run. Product-specific facts (price, size,
compostable time, etc.) belong in that product's own requirement document,
not here.
