Post Generation Requirement — 32 oz TreeSaver™ Bowl
=====================================================

PRODUCT
-------
32 oz TreeSaver™ Bowl — a compostable fiber bowl with PLA lamination,
made from 100% renewable natural resources. Fits pasta, soup, salads,
and sushi; works for both dine-in and takeout/delivery.

KEY BENEFIT TO HIGHLIGHT
-------------------------
Versatile enough to replace multiple single-use containers in a kitchen —
one bowl size that goes from hot pasta straight out of the oven to a
delivery bag without leaking, and composts in a commercial composter in
60–90 days afterward. Lead with "one bowl does it all" versatility, then
back it up with the leak-proof/oven-safe/freezer-safe performance, then
close on the sustainability story.

SUPPORTING FACTS TO DRAW ON
-----------------------------
- Oils and liquids will not soak through.
- Microwave safe, freezer safe, oven-safe (to 425°F).
- PLA lamination keeps sushi from drying out.
- 250 units per case, $76.75/case.
- Pairs with the 32 oz Clear Recycled PET Lid (sold separately, recyclable).
- Biodegradable — composts in a commercial composter in 60–90 days.

TONE
----
Playful-professional — confident and a little upbeat, not preachy. Follow
the Marketing SOP's brand voice (functional benefit first, sustainability
second).

PLATFORMS
---------
Instagram and Facebook — 1–2 images each, square/portrait crop friendly.
RedNote (XHS) if targeting a delivery/catering audience specifically.

CALL TO ACTION
--------------
"Request a Sample" or "Shop Now" — whichever fits the platform's usual CTA
button copy.

WHAT TO AVOID
-------------
- Don't claim the bowl itself is "recyclable" — only the clear PET lid is.
  Keep compostable (bowl) and recyclable (lid) claims separate.
- Don't overload the caption with every spec — pick 1–2 supporting facts
  per post, not the full list above.
